# Unreal: Portfolio: Tell your story

> Legacy lesson, organized for self-study. Screenshots may show an older editor. See the Unreal 5.8 verification scope (legacy attachment omitted) before relying on version-specific steps.

**Be specific and honest about your abilities, skills, and projects.**

Explain who you are, what role(s) you are pursuing, and why someone would hire you.

## Checklist

### Overall

- Include a clear, professional homepage or section with your name, roles, current title (if employed), and details.
- Add professional head-shot or representational avatar.
- Include contact information and links to social media/professional profiles.

### Projects

- Feature 3-5 of your best projects prominently.
- Include high-quality screenshots or promotional images for each project.
- Embed gameplay videos or trailers (30-90 seconds recommended).
- Write clear project descriptions including your role, tools used, and key features
- Highlight specific challenges solved and technical achievements.
- Add playable demos or download links where possible.

### Optional

- Section(s) with the following:
  - A blog or feed of ongoing projects.
  - Game jam or experimental works.
  - Collaborative projects not otherwise included.
