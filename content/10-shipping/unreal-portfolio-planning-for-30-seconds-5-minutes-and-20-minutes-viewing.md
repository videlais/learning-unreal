# Unreal: Portfolio: Planning for 30 seconds, 5 minutes, and 20 minutes viewing

> Legacy lesson, organized for self-study. Screenshots may show an older editor. See the Unreal 5.8 verification scope (legacy attachment omitted) before relying on version-specific steps.

Generally, a portfolio will be reviewed across three time scales:

- First review: 30-60 seconds (skim)
- Deeper review: 5-10 minutes (if you passed first skim)
- Detailed review: 20-30 minutes (if seriously considering you)

Make sure your strongest content is **immediately visible**.

Someone should know within 30 seconds who you are, what role(s) you are pursing, and your specialization(s). If they want to spend more time, they can take the deeper review and play works or read descriptions.
