## *What best shows your work?*

For some projects, having a video or set of screenshots can easily show an external audience your work. However, there are also cases where in-engine capture can be more useful.

Use in-engine screenshots and capture for situations like:

- Demonstrating your workflow and problem-solving approach.
- Highlighting specific features like material networks or complex systems.

Always, regardless of where the screenshot comes from, use annotations or descriptions.

**A screenshot without a written description explaining what a view is seeing and why it matters is nearly worthless!** Why did you include this image? What does it show? Why should an external audience care about the image or project?
