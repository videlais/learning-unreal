# Unreal: Portfolio: How does this project serve as evidence of your skills?

> Legacy lesson, organized for self-study. Screenshots may show an older editor. See the Unreal 5.8 verification scope (legacy attachment omitted) before relying on version-specific steps.

If your include a project in your portfolio, you should generally have:

- Downloadable or link to Itch.io or other platform, if possible. If the project cannot be shared, additional documentation should be included such as a video
- Between 2-4 screenshots with descriptions of what they show, how or if they demonstrate progression, and what challenges they document.
- Context for the project such as:
  - Who as involved?
  - What tools were used?
  - Length of work on the project.

**Every project included should serve as evidence of the skills you claim you have.** If an external audience might doubt, be explicit in the description of the project of what skills or tools were used to help people make the connection.
