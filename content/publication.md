# Publication policy

This public edition contains self-study instructional text, approved course screenshots, authored practice labs and synthetic Blueprint examples.

Excluded: attendance records, assignment settings/rubrics, discussions, institutional metadata, LMS identifiers/links, personal introduction, third-party PDFs/models and undocumented attachments. Old project archives were removed locally and are not reconstructed or uploaded. Unapproved remote illustrations are explicitly omitted, not silently fetched.

The original edition and excluded material are preserved separately in the local session backup, outside this repository. The public build has no dependency on that backup.

[Publication accounting](publication.json) records lesson coverage and omitted-reference categories without publishing private source identifiers.

Legacy screenshots are instructional illustrations, not evidence of Unreal 5.8 reproduction. Read the [compatibility report](compatibility.md) before treating an editor-specific workflow as verified.

Unreal Engine is an Epic Games product. No engine binaries or third-party commercial asset packs are redistributed.
